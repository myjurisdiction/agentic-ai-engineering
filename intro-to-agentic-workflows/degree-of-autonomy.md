### Degrees of Autonomy in Agentic Workflows

- Agentic AI is a spectrum: Agents can have different levels of autonomy rather than simply being classified as agents or non-agents.
- Less Autonomous Agents: Follow predefined, deterministic workflows where the developer controls the sequence of steps and tool calls.
- Semi-Autonomous Agents: LLMs can make some decisions, such as selecting tools and determining certain next steps, within predefined boundaries.
- Highly Autonomous Agents: LLMs independently plan workflows, decide which tools to use, execute tasks, and may even create new tools or functions.
- Trade-offs: Higher autonomy offers more flexibility but introduces unpredictability and makes systems harder to control.

Key Takeaway: Greater autonomy isn't always better. Choose the level of autonomy based on the task's complexity, reliability requirements, and need for control.
